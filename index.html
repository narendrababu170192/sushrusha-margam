A seva-driven movement building a compassionate, value-based, eco-conscious and spiritually awakened society.

Five Core Values

Seva

Selfless service dedicated to society, humanity and all living beings.

Sanskar

Discipline, character, gratitude and values rooted in Bharateeya culture.

Environmental Awareness

Conscious living that protects nature and future generations.

Dharma

Righteousness, responsibility, integrity and harmony in thought and action.

Societal Upliftment

Stronger communities built through unity, compassion and collective responsibility.

About Us

Sushrusha Margam is an independent, non-profit, dharma-based spiritual seva organization dedicated to nurturing disciplined individuals, responsible families and value-based communities through Seva, Dharma, discipline and spiritual awareness.

Know More About Us

Sushrusha Sena

Our Volunteer Wing

Sushrusha Sena is the organized, disciplined volunteer force of Sushrusha Margam. Its men and women train in physical, mental, moral and spiritual discipline for environmental protection, cultural activities, awareness programs and selfless service.
