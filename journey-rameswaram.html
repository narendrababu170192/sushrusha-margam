A spiritually enriching pilgrimage from Hyderabad through the sacred temple towns of Kumbakonam, Rameswaram, Kanchipuram and Tirupati, seeking the blessings of Lord Shiva, Goddess Shakti and Lord Venkateswara.

Day 1 – 29 July | Hyderabad → Kumbakonam

  • Departure from Hyderabad, journey towards Kumbakonam.
  • Arrival and accommodation check-in; evening temple visit, subject to time availability.
  • Overnight stay at Kumbakonam.

Day 2 – 30 July | Kumbakonam Temple Darshan

  • Morning temple visits in and around Kumbakonam; darshan at important Shiva and Devi temples.
  • Devotional activities and personal prayer. Overnight stay at Kumbakonam.

Day 3 – 31 July | Kumbakonam → Rameswaram

  • Morning departure towards Rameswaram; arrival and hotel check-in.
  • Evening visit to Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple, subject to temple timings. Overnight at Rameswaram.

Day 4 – 01 August | Rameswaram – Sacred Temple Darshan

  • Early morning Sri Ramanathaswamy Darshan; traditional temple rituals subject to availability.
  • Visit Agni Theertham and other pilgrimage locations. Overnight at Rameswaram.

Day 5 – 02 August | Rameswaram → Kanchipuram

  • Morning departure towards Kanchipuram, the historic temple city.
  • Evening visit to Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple, subject to timings. Overnight at Kanchipuram.

Day 6 – 03 August | Kanchipuram → Tirupati

  • Morning temple darshan in Kanchipuram; departure towards Tirupati.
  • Evening local temple visit / devotional activities. Overnight at Tirupati.

Day 7 – 04 August | Tirupati – Tirumala Darshan

  • Early morning journey to Tirumala; darshan of Sri Venkateswara Swamy.
  • Return to Tirupati; evening rest and personal prayer.

Day 8 – 05 August | Tirupati → Hyderabad

  • Morning local visit / temple darshan, subject to available time.
  • Return journey to Hyderabad; yatra concludes with the blessings of Lord Venkateswara.

Major Temple Darshans

  • Kumbakonam – Sacred Temple Heritage
  • Rameswaram – Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple
  • Agni Theertham
  • Kanchipuram – Temple City
  • Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple
  • Tirumala – Sri Venkateswara Swamy Darshan
Note: Darshan, temple entry, pooja / seva and special-entry arrangements are subject to the respective temple authorities, their availability, timings, regulations and booking policies. The itinerary may be adjusted due to travel / transport schedules, weather, local conditions, temple restrictions or other unforeseen circumstances.
