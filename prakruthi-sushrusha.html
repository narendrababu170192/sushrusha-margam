Prakruthi Sushrusha

Nature-centered seva inspired by Dharma and responsible living.

Purpose

Prakruthi Sushrusha encourages children, families and communities to develop a grateful and responsible relationship with nature. We connect learning with the Pancha Bhoothas and inspire sustainable action through community participation.

Activities

  • Plastic-free awareness campaigns
  • Cloth bag distribution and responsible consumption awareness
  • One sapling plantation during Akshara Abhyaasam
  • Connecting child growth with tree growth
  • Environmental responsibility learning for children
  • Tree plantation and community clean-up seva

Aksharabyaasam Begins Education – Vriksham Begins Life

Our Vision

Every tree planted is a symbol of gratitude, growth and responsibility. By blending traditional values with environmental stewardship, we seek a greener, healthier world for future generations.
