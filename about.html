About Sushrusha Margam

A life journey guided by Dharma, Seva, discipline and spiritual awareness.

Who We Are

Sushrusha Margam is an independent, non-profit, dharma-based spiritual seva organization dedicated to nurturing disciplined individuals, responsible families and value-based communities. “Sushrusha” means selfless service offered with sincerity, compassion and responsibility. “Margam” means a path or way of life.

Inspired by Sanatana Dharma, Bharateeya culture and Shanmukha Tattvam, we help people live with purpose, serve with humility, protect nature, preserve culture and contribute to the welfare of society.

Our Vision and Mission

Vision

To create a spiritually awakened, dharma-conscious society through disciplined seva, inner sadhana and cultural revival.

Mission

To build a structured volunteer force, revive Shanmukha Tattvam through daily practice, empower citizens through guidance, reconnect families with Sanatana Dharma and organize seva programs from local to national level.

Purpose and Values

Our work connects spiritual growth with societal need. Seva, Sanskar, environmental awareness, Dharma and societal upliftment guide our decisions and activities.

  • Selfless service for society, humanity and all living beings
  • Discipline, character, gratitude and Bharateeya values
  • Responsible living that protects nature and future generations
  • Righteousness, integrity and harmony in thought and action
  • Community strength through unity, compassion and responsibility

Activities and Community Engagement

Our volunteers, families, educational partners and community supporters participate in tree plantation, plastic-free awareness, cloth-bag distribution, Chithjyothi Sadhana, cultural programs, temple-connected family activities and leadership development.

Major Initiatives

Prakruthi Sushrusha nurtures nature. Akshara Vriksha Samskar connects education with environmental responsibility. Bharathaalaya Kutumba Samskara Seva strengthens families, temples and cultural values.
