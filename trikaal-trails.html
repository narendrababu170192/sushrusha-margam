Rameswaram – Kumbakonam – Kanchipuram – Tirupati Yatra

29 July 2026 – 05 August 2026 · 8 Days / 7 Nights · ₹19,999 per person

A spiritually enriching pilgrimage from Hyderabad through the sacred temple towns of Kumbakonam, Rameswaram, Kanchipuram and Tirupati, seeking the blessings of Lord Shiva, Goddess Shakti and Lord Venkateswara.

Day 1 – 29 July | Hyderabad → Kumbakonam

  • Departure from Hyderabad, journey towards Kumbakonam.
  • Arrival and accommodation check-in; evening temple visit, subject to time availability.
  • Overnight stay at Kumbakonam.

Day 2 – 30 July | Kumbakonam Temple Darshan

  • Morning temple visits in and around Kumbakonam; darshan at important Shiva and Devi temples.
  • Devotional activities and personal prayer. Overnight stay at Kumbakonam.

Day 3 – 31 July | Kumbakonam → Rameswaram

  • Morning departure towards Rameswaram; arrival and hotel check-in.
  • Evening visit to Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple, subject to temple timings. Overnight at Rameswaram.

Day 4 – 01 August | Rameswaram – Sacred Temple Darshan

  • Early morning Sri Ramanathaswamy Darshan; traditional temple rituals subject to availability.
  • Visit Agni Theertham and other pilgrimage locations. Overnight at Rameswaram.

Day 5 – 02 August | Rameswaram → Kanchipuram

  • Morning departure towards Kanchipuram, the historic temple city.
  • Evening visit to Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple, subject to timings. Overnight at Kanchipuram.

Day 6 – 03 August | Kanchipuram → Tirupati

  • Morning temple darshan in Kanchipuram; departure towards Tirupati.
  • Evening local temple visit / devotional activities. Overnight at Tirupati.

Day 7 – 04 August | Tirupati – Tirumala Darshan

  • Early morning journey to Tirumala; darshan of Sri Venkateswara Swamy.
  • Return to Tirupati; evening rest and personal prayer.

Day 8 – 05 August | Tirupati → Hyderabad

  • Morning local visit / temple darshan, subject to available time.
  • Return journey to Hyderabad; yatra concludes with the blessings of Lord Venkateswara.

Yatra Highlights

  • Kumbakonam – Sacred Temple Heritage
  • Rameswaram – Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple
  • Agni Theertham
  • Kanchipuram – Temple City
  • Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple
  • Tirumala – Sri Venkateswara Swamy Darshan
  • Guided pilgrimage and travel support
Note: Temple darshan, pooja/seva and special-entry arrangements are subject to the respective temple authorities, availability, timings and regulations. The sequence of visits may vary depending on travel schedules, temple timings, weather, local conditions and other unforeseen circumstances.

Thiruvannamalai – Kanchi – Tirupati Yatra

14 August 2026 – 18 August 2026 · 5 Days / 4 Nights · ₹14,999 per person

Begin your pilgrimage from Hyderabad and journey through three sacred destinations of South India — Thiruvannamalai, Kanchipuram and Tirupati. Experience the divine presence of Lord Arunachaleswara, seek the blessings of Sri Kamakshi Amman and conclude your journey at the sacred hills of Lord Venkateswara.

Day 1 – 14 August | Hyderabad → Thiruvannamalai

  • Departure from Hyderabad; travel towards Thiruvannamalai. Arrival and hotel check-in.
  • Evening visit to Sri Arunachaleswarar Temple; darshan of Lord Arunachaleswara and Unnamalai Amman. Overnight stay.

Day 2 – 15 August | Thiruvannamalai – Arunachala Darshan

  • Early morning temple darshan; visit important sacred places around Arunachala.
  • Opportunity for Girivalam (circumambulation of Arunachala Hill), subject to schedule. Visit Ramana Maharshi Ashram, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 3 – 16 August | Thiruvannamalai → Kanchipuram

  • Morning departure for Kanchipuram; visit Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple.
  • Darshan at important Shiva and Vishnu temples, subject to time. Continue onward towards Tirupati. Overnight stay.

Day 4 – 17 August | Tirupati – Tirumala Darshan

  • Early morning journey to Tirumala; darshan of Sri Venkateswara Swamy.
  • Visit important temples and sacred places on Tirumala, subject to time and access. Return to Tirupati; overnight stay.

Day 5 – 18 August | Tirupati → Hyderabad

  • Morning local visit / temple darshan at Tirupati, subject to time.
  • Begin return journey to Hyderabad. Yatra concludes with the blessings of Lord Venkateswara.

Yatra Highlights

  • Sri Arunachaleswarar Temple, Thiruvannamalai
  • Sacred Arunachala Hill
  • Girivalam opportunity
  • Kanchipuram temple visit
  • Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple
  • Tirumala – Sri Venkateswara Swamy Darshan
  • Spiritual guidance and coordinated pilgrimage support
Note: Temple darshan, pooja/seva and special-entry arrangements are subject to availability and the respective temple authorities' rules. The sequence of visits may be adjusted according to travel conditions, temple timings, crowd conditions and local circumstances.

Puri – Tarapith – Kamakhya Yatra

22 August 2026 – 30 August 2026 · 9 Days / 8 Nights · ₹29,999 per person

From the shores of Puri to the sacred Shakti Peetha of Kamakhya, this journey takes you through some of Eastern India's most revered spiritual destinations — Visakhapatnam, Puri, Tarapith and Kamakhya.

Day 1 – 22 August | Hyderabad → Visakhapatnam

  • Departure from Hyderabad; journey towards Visakhapatnam. Arrival and check-in.
  • Visit a selected temple / spiritual place in Visakhapatnam, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 2 – 23 August | Visakhapatnam → Puri

  • Morning departure towards Puri, Odisha; arrival and hotel check-in.
  • Evening visit to Jagannath Temple surroundings, subject to temple timings. Overnight stay.

Day 3 – 24 August | Puri – Jagannath Darshan

  • Early morning temple visit; Sri Jagannath Swamy Darshan, subject to temple rules.
  • Visit important sacred places in Puri. Overnight stay.

Day 4 – 25 August | Puri → Tarapith

  • Morning departure from Puri towards Tarapith, West Bengal; arrival and check-in.
  • Evening visit to Maa Tara Temple, subject to temple timings. Overnight stay.

Day 5 – 26 August | Tarapith → Kamakhya

  • Morning darshan at Maa Tara Temple, subject to schedule; begin journey towards Guwahati / Kamakhya.
  • Arrival at Kamakhya and hotel check-in. Overnight stay.

Day 6 – 27 August | Kamakhya – Maa Kamakhya Darshan

  • Early morning visit to Maa Kamakhya Temple; darshan at the sacred Kamakhya Shakti Peetha.
  • Explore important temples within the Kamakhya complex, subject to time and access. Overnight stay.

Day 7 – 28 August | Kamakhya – Nilachal Hill Spiritual Circuit

  • Continue the pilgrimage around the sacred Kamakhya complex; visit selected Mahavidya-related shrines.
  • Personal prayer, meditation and devotional activities. Overnight stay.

Day 8 – 29 August | Kamakhya → Howrah

  • Morning departure from Kamakhya; journey towards Howrah / Kolkata.
  • Arrival and local visit, subject to available time. Overnight / onward travel as per schedule.

Day 9 – 30 August | Howrah → Hyderabad

  • Final morning in Howrah / Kolkata, subject to travel schedule.
  • Begin return journey to Hyderabad. Yatra concludes with the blessings of Maa Kamakhya and the Divine Mother.

Yatra Highlights

  • Visakhapatnam
  • Puri – Sri Jagannath Swamy Darshan
  • Tarapith – Maa Tara Darshan
  • Kamakhya – Sacred Shakti Peetha
  • Selected temples of the Kamakhya complex
  • Mahavidya-related sacred places, subject to access and time
  • Howrah / Kolkata
  • Guided pilgrimage support throughout the journey
Important Note: Darshan, temple entry, pooja/seva and special arrangements are subject to the respective temple authorities, their availability, timings, regulations and booking policies. The itinerary may be adjusted due to train/transport schedules, weather, local conditions, temple restrictions or other unforeseen circumstances.

About Us – Trikaal Trails

Walk the Sacred Path, Experience the Divine

Trikaal Trails is more than travel – it is a thoughtfully curated pilgrimage experience designed to help devotees connect with the spiritual essence of India's sacred temples. Every trail combines temple history, traditions, legends, rituals and practical guidance, allowing pilgrims to undertake their yatra with devotion, knowledge and confidence. Trikaal Trails provides safe, comfortable and dependable transportation for pilgrims across India. Whether you are travelling individually, with family or in a group, we ensure a hassle-free journey.

A pilgrimage is more than reaching a temple – it is a sacred journey of devotion, preparation and transformation. Our intention is to simplify sacred journeys by providing authentic pilgrimage guidance, darshan planning, ritual assistance and reliable travel support while preserving the sanctity of every temple tradition. We believe devotees should spend their time in prayer and spiritual reflection, not worrying about planning, bookings or logistics.

Trikaal Trails – Trikaal Spiritual & Travel Services began with a simple vision to serve pilgrims and long-distance travellers. Under the leadership of Mr. Durga Prasad, we expanded our offerings to provide comprehensive spiritual and pilgrimage support across India.

Our Philosophy

Travel takes you to a destination.

A pilgrimage transforms the devotee.

Trikaal Trails exists not merely to help you reach a temple, but to help you experience its spiritual essence.

Our Mission

To provide dependable spiritual and travel services that make every pilgrimage simple, comfortable and spiritually fulfilling through trusted guidance, professional coordination and dedicated customer support.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted pilgrimage service providers by connecting devotees with sacred temples through reliable darshan assistance, spiritual services and well-planned travel experiences.
