An eight-day eastern pilgrimage linking the coastal kshetras of Andhra and Odisha with Puri Jagannath, the temples of Bhubaneswar and Kolkata, and the sacred Kamakhya Shakti Peetha of Assam.

Day 1 | Pithapuram → Annavaram

  • Darshan of Sri Kukkuteswara Swamy and Pada Gaya kshetra at Pithapuram.
  • Travel to Annavaram; darshan of Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy, subject to timings. Overnight stay.

Day 2 | Annavaram → Simhachalam → Visakhapatnam

  • Darshan of Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy at Simhachalam.
  • Continue to Visakhapatnam; overnight stay.

Day 3 | Arasavelli → Srikurmam → onward

  • Darshan of Sri Suryanarayana Swamy at Arasavelli.
  • Darshan of Sri Kurmanatha Swamy at Srikurmam. Board train / coach towards Odisha; overnight journey.

Day 4 | Puri – Jagannath Darshan

  • Arrival at Puri; hotel check-in.
  • Sri Jagannath Swamy darshan and visit to Sakshi Gopal, subject to temple rules. Overnight stay.

Day 5 | Puri → Bhubaneswar → Kolkata

  • Visit Lingaraja and other Bhubaneswar temples, subject to time.
  • Continue towards Kolkata by train; overnight stay.

Day 6 | Kolkata → Guwahati

  • Morning visit to Kalighat / Dakshineswar, subject to time.
  • Begin journey towards Guwahati / Kamakhya; overnight journey.

Day 7 | Kamakhya Darshan

  • Early morning darshan at the Kamakhya Shakti Peetha.
  • Visit Umananda (Peacock Island) and temples of the Kamakhya complex, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 8 | Vodyana Girija Devi → Return

  • Darshan of Girija Devi at Vodyana / Biswanath, subject to schedule.
  • Begin return journey. Yatra concludes with the blessings of the Divine Mother.
