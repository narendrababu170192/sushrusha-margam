An eight-day grand temple pilgrimage across Tamil Nadu, weaving together the Devi, Shiva and Vishnu kshetras — Kanchipuram, Thiruvannamalai, Chidambaram, Kumbakonam, Thanjavur, Srirangam, Rameswaram and Madurai.

Day 1 | Arrival → Kanchipuram

  • Arrival at Chennai; drive to Kanchipuram.
  • Darshan at Sri Kamakshi Amman and Sri Ekambareswarar temples, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 2 | Kanchipuram → Thiruvannamalai → Chidambaram

  • Morning departure to Thiruvannamalai; darshan of Sri Arunachaleswarar.
  • Continue to Chidambaram; evening at leisure. Overnight stay.

Day 3 | Chidambaram → Kumbakonam

  • Darshan of Sri Nataraja at Chidambaram.
  • Travel to Kumbakonam; darshan of Sri Kumbeshwarar, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 4 | Kumbakonam → Thanjavur → Trichy

  • Temple visits around Kumbakonam, subject to time.
  • Darshan of Sri Brihadeeswarar (Big Temple) at Thanjavur. Continue to Trichy / Srirangam. Overnight stay.

Day 5 | Srirangam Darshan → Rameswaram

  • Darshan of Sri Ranganathaswamy at Srirangam.
  • Travel to Rameswaram; evening visit to Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple, subject to timings. Overnight stay.

Day 6 | Rameswaram

  • Early morning Sri Ramanathaswamy darshan and theertham snanam, subject to availability.
  • Visit Agni Theertham and Dhanushkodi, subject to conditions. Overnight stay.

Day 7 | Rameswaram → Madurai

  • Morning departure to Madurai; darshan of Sri Meenakshi Amman.
  • Evening temple aarti, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 8 | Madurai → Departure

  • Morning local temple visit, subject to time.
  • Begin return journey. Yatra concludes with the blessings of the divine temples of Tamil Nadu.
