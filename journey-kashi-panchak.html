A focused five-day Kashi pilgrimage — the Ganga Aarti, Sri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga, Annapurna Devi, the Vishalakshi Shakti Peetha, Kalabhairav, Sankat Mochan Hanuman, Kedareshwar and the Buddhist kshetra of Sarnath.

Day 1 | Arrival → Varanasi

  • Arrival at Varanasi; hotel check-in.
  • Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat. Overnight stay.

Day 2 | Kashi Vishwanath Darshan

  • Early morning boat ride on the Ganga, subject to conditions.
  • Darshan of Sri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga, Sri Annapurna Devi and Sri Vishalakshi (Shakti Peetha). Overnight stay.

Day 3 | Kalabhairav & Kashi Temples

  • Darshan at Sri Kalabhairav Temple.
  • Visit Sri Sankat Mochan Hanuman and Sri Kedareshwar (Kedar Ghat) temples. Overnight stay.

Day 4 | Sarnath & Panchakroshi

  • Visit Sarnath — Dhamek Stupa and museum.
  • Darshan at additional temples on the Panchakroshi route, subject to time. Overnight stay.

Day 5 | Departure

  • Morning darshan at Sri Kashi Vishwanath, subject to time.
  • Begin return journey. Yatra concludes with the blessings of Kashi Vishwanath.

Major Temple Darshans

  • Sri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga
  • Sri Annapurna Devi
  • Sri Vishalakshi Shakti Peetha
  • Sri Kalabhairav
  • Sri Sankat Mochan Hanuman
  • Sri Kedareshwar (Kedar Ghat)
  • Sarnath
Note: Darshan, temple entry, pooja / seva and special-entry arrangements are subject to the respective temple authorities, their availability, timings, regulations and booking policies. The itinerary may be adjusted due to travel / transport schedules, weather, local conditions, temple restrictions or other unforeseen circumstances.
