✓ Asia✓ India✓ Culture✓ Heritage✓ Religious✓ Spiritual✓ Hills

A carefully planned South India circuit from Hyderabad — Sri Varasiddhi Vinayaka at Kanipakam, the temple city of Kanchipuram, the Girivalam of Arunachala, Tirumala Sri Venkateswara and the Rahu-Ketu kshetra of Srikalahasti.

Day 1 | Hyderabad → Kanipakam → Kanchipuram

  • Departure from Hyderabad; travel towards Kanipakam.
  • Darshan of Sri Varasiddhi Vinayaka Swamy, subject to temple timings.
  • Continue to Kanchipuram; hotel check-in and overnight stay.

Day 2 | Kanchipuram Temple Darshan

  • Darshan at Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple.
  • Darshan at Sri Ekambareswarar and Sri Varadaraja Perumal temples, subject to time.
  • Overnight stay at Kanchipuram.

Day 3 | Kanchipuram → Arunachalam

  • Morning departure towards Thiruvannamalai (Arunachalam).
  • Evening darshan of Sri Arunachaleswarar; opportunity for Girivalam, subject to schedule.
  • Overnight stay at Arunachalam.

Day 4 | Arunachalam → Tirupati

  • Morning temple darshan and sacred places around Arunachala.
  • Travel to Tirupati; overnight stay.

Day 5 | Tirumala Darshan → Srikalahasti → Hyderabad

  • Early morning Tirumala Sri Venkateswara Swamy darshan.
  • Darshan at Sri Kalahasteeswara Temple (Rahu-Ketu kshetra), subject to time.
  • Begin return journey to Hyderabad.

Major Temple Darshans

  • Sri Varasiddhi Vinayaka, Kanipakam
  • Kanchi Sri Kamakshi Amman
  • Sri Ekambareswarar, Kanchipuram
  • Sri Varadaraja Perumal, Kanchipuram
  • Sri Arunachaleswarar, Thiruvannamalai
  • Tirumala Sri Venkateswara Swamy
  • Sri Kalahasteeswara, Srikalahasti
Note: Darshan, temple entry, pooja / seva and special-entry arrangements are subject to the respective temple authorities, their availability, timings, regulations and booking policies. The itinerary may be adjusted due to travel / transport schedules, weather, local conditions, temple restrictions or other unforeseen circumstances.
