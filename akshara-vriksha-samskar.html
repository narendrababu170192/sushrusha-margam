Akshara Vriksha Samskar

Growing education, values and nature together.

Purpose

Akshara Vriksha Samskar connects Akshara Abhyaasam, the first step of education, with planting a sapling. Children and families learn that knowledge and nature must grow together through gratitude, care and responsibility.

Activities

  • One sapling plantation during Akshara Abhyaasam
  • Connecting child growth with tree growth
  • Environmental responsibility learning for children
  • Plastic-free and cloth-bag awareness
  • Community participation in sustainable initiatives

Aksharabyaasam Begins Education – Vriksham Begins Life

For Families and Schools

We invite schools, families and community partners to make education a lived practice of values, care for nature and service to society.
